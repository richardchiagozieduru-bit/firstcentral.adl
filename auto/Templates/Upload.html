File Submission & Compliance Guidelines
FirstCentral Credit Bureau Upload Specifications
Accepted File Types

Single workbook containing all templates, or multiple files named after each sheet type.

.xlsx .xls .xlsb .xlsm .csv / .txt
Monthly Reporting Cycle

Credit bureau submissions are in-arrears: report previous closed month (e.g. August data submitted in September). One authoritative filing is accepted per organization cycle.

Password & Format Rules
Password Protected: Encrypted Excel workbooks are supported—enter your file password below before uploading.
No Mixed Types: Do not mix CSV and Excel in the same batch.
Mandatory Sheets (Minimum 2)
Required Borrower Template: Individual Borrower OR Corporate Borrower.
Required Credit Information: Loan facilities, balances, classification, repayment terms.
Optional Enrichment Sheets
Optional Principal Officers: Directors and executives for corporate facilities.
Optional Guarantors: Third-party facility guarantors and collateral details.
Key Columns & Matching
Customer ID: Must be identical across Borrower and Credit sheets to link facilities.
Account Number: Unique facility identifier required for all credit records.
Pre-Merged Files Supported (Single File)

If submitting a pre-merged dataset named consumermerged or commercialmerged, it is accepted as a single self-contained file (both borrower and credit data combined). The ACCOUNTNUMBER column is mandatory, and you will be prompted to choose automatic entity splitting upon upload.
